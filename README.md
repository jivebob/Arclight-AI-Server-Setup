
Arclight-AI-Server-Setup
This repository documents the process of deploying and operating an Arclight Engines AI inference server, including installation procedures, configuration examples, troubleshooting guidance, and optimisation recommendations gathered during real-world deployment.

Before You Begin
A quick word of warning: I am not a code writer, at least not for Nix or C++. Hell, I can't even do a "Hello, World!" at present. My actual languages are HTML, CSS, CSSW3, and Java.

The code provided in this guide was generated by AI and many nights yelling at my screen. All the hardware modifications were, of course, my own, and the entire scope of the project was guided by me.

Getting to the point, this proved to be a very difficult setup to get working. If you are looking for something that will get you up and running in an hour's time, or even a weekend, then unless you have some serious technical chops, this is the wrong repo for you.

BIOS Notice
At present, I am unable to provide a copy of the BIOS used in this project. The firmware was obtained separately, and I do not currently have a verified redistribution source or version number available.

The system was built using an Intel Server Board S2600CO. If you are attempting to replicate this setup, I recommend searching for the final firmware releases issued for the S2600CO platform through archived Intel resources, community forums, or firmware preservation projects.

During my research, the most recent firmware package I was able to identify for the S2600CO platform was System BIOS 02.06.0005. The package also included updated Management Engine, BMC, and FRUSDR components.

As Intel has discontinued support for this platform and many of the original download links are no longer available, obtaining the firmware may require some digging through community archives and historical mirrors. Please verify that any firmware you obtain is intended for your specific board revision before performing an update.

I cannot verify the safety or authenticity of firmware obtained from third-party sources. Flashing server firmware always carries a risk of rendering the hardware unusable. Proceed at your own risk.

Support
If you encounter issues while following this guide, please open an Issue.

I may not be able to respond immediately, as life has a habit of getting in the way, but I will do my best to reply when time permits.

Please include as much relevant information as possible, including hardware specifications, error messages, log output, and the point in the guide where things went wrong. The more information you provide, the easier it will be to troubleshoot the problem.
